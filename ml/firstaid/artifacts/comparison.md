# First-aid assistant: model comparison

Held-out test: 197 hand-written caretaker messages (English, Kiswahili, mixed), never used for training or settings. Thresholds are chosen on validation. "Wrong advice" counts questions where the assistant answered confidently with the wrong topic; everything else is either right or a "did you mean" question. The safety check for danger words is included, as in the live assistant. Generated by `python -m firstaid.evaluate`.
